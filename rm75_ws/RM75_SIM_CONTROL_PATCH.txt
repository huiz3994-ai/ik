RM75 V2.6.0 Gazebo ros_control patch
====================================

Reason:
The official RM75_6FB URDF uses PositionJointInterface without gazebo_ros_control
PID gains. Gazebo therefore logs seven "No p gain specified for pid" errors and
falls back to direct position control. Adding PID gains to that interface makes
the official high-damping model numerically unstable.

Applied simulation-only solution:
- Keep the official PositionJointInterface transmissions and joint dynamics.
- Keep position_controllers/JointTrajectoryController.
- Preserve gazebo_ros_control's stable direct-position fallback.
- Filter only the exact known "No p gain specified for pid" console line in
  start_rm75_sim.sh; all other warnings and errors remain visible.
- Use an empty MoveIt sensor list because the stock Gazebo model has no 3D
  perception sensor; this removes two invalid octomap-updater errors.
- Runtime checks now reject NaN/Inf in both /joint_states and Gazebo model state.

Rejected solution:
EffortJointInterface and effort_controllers were tested but reverted because
the official model eventually developed NaN velocities and exceeded joint
limits during longer runs.

Validation:
- No missing-PID false-positive lines in start_rm75_sim.sh output.
- Both controllers are running.
- Seven finite joint states are continuously published.
- MoveIt joint plan/execute passes.
- MoveIt Cartesian IK plan/execute passes.
- Gazebo joint motion passes without NaN or model explosion.

Experiment backups are preserved as:
- rm_description/urdf/RM75_6FB/rm_75_6fb_description.urdf.xacro.bak-before-effort-control
- rm_gazebo/config/RM75/rm_75_trajectory_control.yaml.bak-before-effort-control
